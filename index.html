About

Curating elegant narratives across fashion, culture, and celebration.

Entrepreneur • Creative Director • Fashion Visionary • Wedding Curator

Nandini Kumar is a name synonymous with creativity, elegance, and innovation. With over 25 years of experience across fashion, media, entertainment, and luxury, she has built a distinguished career at the intersection of design, storytelling, and strategic brand creation. Her academic foundation in Science and Mathematics, formal training in Fashion Design in Mumbai, and entrepreneurial education from IIM Bangalore have shaped a unique perspective that seamlessly blends artistic vision with business excellence. A pioneer in bringing fashion and lifestyle storytelling into Indian homes through television, Nandini has conceptualised and delivered creative projects across television, films, documentaries, advertising, fashion, and experiential platforms. Throughout her journey, she has collaborated with some of India's most respected brands, including Rocky Star, FedEx, Paco Rabanne, Parle-G, Roopam, Shoppers Stop, LIC of India, Jashn Sarees, and Kerala Tourism.
